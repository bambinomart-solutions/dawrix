Delete Account & Data

Dawrix — Alarm & Morning Ritual · published by bambinomart.solutions

Dawrix does not require an account, and Google Sign-In (if you use it) is optional and stored only on your device — Dawrix has no server-side account or database to delete anything from.

Delete everything on your device

  • In the app: Settings → Account → "Delete Account & Data". This disconnects Google Sign-In (if signed in) and permanently erases all alarms, history, wellness records and settings from your device immediately.
  • Without opening the app: Android Settings → Apps → Dawrix → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app.

Request deletion another way

If you cannot use the app and want to confirm deletion or ask a question, email bambinomart.solutions@gmail.com from the address associated with your request, and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Google Account data

Signing out of Google within Dawrix does not delete your Google Account itself. To manage or delete your Google Account, visit myaccount.google.com.
