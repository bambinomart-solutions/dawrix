Privacy Policy

Dawrix — Alarm & Morning Ritual · Effective date: 5 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Dawrix alarm app ("Dawrix", "we", "us"), published by bambinomart.solutions, an individual developer based in India, handles, why, and the choices you have. Dawrix does not require an account: every alarm feature works fully without signing in, and everything you create in the app stays on your device. The only data that ever leaves your device is handled by Google — the optional Google Sign-In, Google Play Billing for subscriptions, and, for users on the free plan, Google AdMob for ads.

1. Data stored only on your device

The following is stored locally in the app's private storage on your phone. It is never sent to us or to any third party:

  • Alarms you create: time, repeat days, label, emoji, colour, ringtone choice, snooze and morning-ritual settings.
  • Alarm history: when each alarm rang, how it was dismissed or snoozed (last 200 entries).
  • Morning ritual records: your daily Wake Readiness Score, ritual completion, and the optional intention you type (last 90 days).
  • App settings (theme, snooze defaults, default ringtone) and a cached copy of your subscription status.
  • Your Google account name, email address and profile photo URL, only if you choose to sign in (see section 2) — used solely to display your name in Settings.

Dawrix does not use the microphone, camera, location, contacts, motion sensors, photos or files.

2. Optional Google Sign-In

Settings includes an optional "Sign in with Google" button. It is never required — every feature of the app, including Pro, works fully without it.

  • If you sign in, Google shares your name, email address, profile photo URL and Google account ID with the app.
  • This information is stored only on your device, to show your name and photo in Settings. It is never sent to us or to any other third party.
  • Signing out (Settings → Account → Sign Out) removes it from the device immediately.

Google's own handling of your account is governed by the Google Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy).

3. Subscriptions (Google Play Billing)

Dawrix Pro subscriptions are sold and processed entirely by Google Play. Your payment details are handled by Google, never by us. On your device the app receives from Google Play only the product purchased and its status, which it uses to unlock Pro features. This information is not sent to us. Google's processing is governed by the Google Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy).

4. Advertising (free plan only)

The free version of Dawrix shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Pro subscribers do not see ads. To serve, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and sends to Google:

  • Device identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID and App Set ID.
  • IP address, from which approximate (coarse) location may be derived.
  • Ad interaction data (ad impressions and taps) and app information.
  • Diagnostic data such as SDK performance and crash information.

Google uses this data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention as described in "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK, Switzerland and certain US states) the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Legal → Ad privacy choices, and you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads.

5. Permissions

Dawrix requests only what an alarm clock needs:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) and full-screen alarms (USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT) — to show the ringing alarm, including over the lock screen.
  • Exact alarms (USE_EXACT_ALARM / SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM) — to ring at exactly the time you set.
  • Foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE) — to keep the alarm sound playing until you dismiss it.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) — to restore your alarms after the phone restarts.
  • Wake lock and vibration (WAKE_LOCK, VIBRATE) — to wake the device and vibrate while ringing.
  • Internet and network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE), Google Play billing (BILLING) and Advertising ID (AD_ID) — for subscriptions and ads.

6. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your data and we do not receive it. Apart from the Google Mobile Ads SDK described in section 4 (free plan) and Google Play Billing described in section 3, no data is shared with any third party. Dawrix contains no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs of its own.

7. Retention and account deletion

  • Alarm history keeps the last 200 entries; morning ritual records keep the last 90 days. Older entries are deleted automatically.
  • You can delete any single alarm in the app at any time.

To delete your account and all associated data:

  • In-app: Settings → Account → "Delete Account & Data". This disconnects Google Sign-In (if used) and permanently erases all alarms, history, wellness records and settings from your device immediately.
  • Online: bambinomart-solutions.github.io/dawrix/delete-account.html — a web page you can use to request deletion without opening the app, or to reach us directly.
  • You can also delete all app data via Android Settings → Apps → Dawrix → Storage → Clear storage, or by uninstalling the app.

Data Google itself collects for the optional sign-in or for ads is retained and deleted under Google's own policies; you can manage your Google Account at myaccount.google.com, and reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads.

8. Security

App data is kept in Android's private app storage, which other apps cannot read, and is protected by your device's screen lock and encryption. Communication with Google Play and Google AdMob is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) by Google's SDKs. App backups are disabled, so your data is not copied to cloud backups.

9. Children

Dawrix is intended for people aged 18 and over. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. Ads in Dawrix are not child-directed. If you believe a child has used the app, you can delete all data as described in section 7 or contact us at the address in section 13.

10. Health and wellness information

The Wake Readiness Score and morning ritual (hydration reminder, breathing exercise, daily intention) are general wellness features, not medical advice. The score is calculated on your device only from snoozes, challenge completion time and dismissal time. It is not shared.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above and, for material changes, notify you in the app. The current version is always available in the app and at the address listed on the Google Play store listing.

12. Your rights and choices

Dawrix has no servers or user accounts of its own, so almost everything we hold about you is on your device and under your control. You can:

  • Access, correct or delete your data: view and edit your alarms and settings in the app; erase everything with Settings → Account → "Delete Account & Data" (or "Delete My Data").
  • Withdraw consent: sign out of Google in Settings → Account, change your ad choices in Settings → Privacy & Legal → Ad privacy choices (shown where required), and reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Ask us a question or make a request about data handled by Dawrix at the address in section 13; we will acknowledge it within 7 days and respond within 30 days.
  • For data that Google itself holds (your Google Account, Google Play purchases, advertising data), exercise your rights with Google at myaccount.google.com or policies.google.com/privacy.

India (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023): Dawrix processes personal data only with your consent (ads consent form, optional Google Sign-In) or to provide a service you request (subscriptions through Google Play). Where the Act applies you have the right to access information about, correct, complete, update and erase your personal data, to withdraw consent, to grievance redressal (section 13), and to nominate another person to exercise these rights.

European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland (GDPR / UK GDPR): our legal bases are consent (ads and optional Sign-In) and performance of a contract (Dawrix Pro, processed by Google Play). You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, the right to withdraw consent at any time, and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority. Data processed by Google for ads may be transferred outside your country under Google's own safeguards.

United States: we do not sell personal information and we do not receive your personal information. Ads are served by Google under its own terms; where a US state privacy law gives you a right to opt out of targeted advertising, you can use Settings → Privacy & Legal → Ad privacy choices (where shown) and your device's Ads settings.
