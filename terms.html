1. What Dawrix does

Every alarm in Dawrix rings at the set time, and to dismiss it you must first complete a short randomly chosen brain game (word unscramble, colour sequence, typing, number grid, colour match, spot the lookalike, block stacking or reaction targets). Snooze is a Dawrix Pro feature: subscribers can turn it on and choose the length (5 or 10 minutes) and how many snoozes are allowed (1 to 3). The free plan has no snooze.

After an alarm is dismissed in the morning, Dawrix Pro can guide you through an optional morning ritual (hydration, box breathing, setting an intention) and shows a Wake Readiness Score.

2. Emergency stop

Any ringing alarm can be stopped without completing the challenge. Double-tap the clock and label area at the top of the alarm screen — or tap the screen ten times in quick succession to reveal an Emergency stop button — then wait out the 5-second countdown and type the short random 10-character sequence shown. The alarm keeps ringing until the sequence is typed correctly. In a real emergency, always contact emergency services first.
